How to Use the Design Tool

Everything you need to design your landscape

🛒
Adding Items

Open the Products panel on the left and click any item — it will be placed on your canvas. Scroll through categories (Shrubs, Grass, Hardscapes, Paths, Furniture…) to find what you need.

✋
Moving Items

Click an item to select it, then drag it to reposition anywhere on the canvas.

↔️
Resizing Items

Select an item — white squares appear at each corner. Drag any corner to make the item larger or smaller.

🔄
Rotating Items

Select an item — a green circle appears above it on a stem. Drag that circle in any direction to rotate the item. The rotation follows your cursor like a wheel.

📋
Front & Back (Layers)

When items overlap, use the Front and Back buttons in the control bar above a selected item to control which one appears on top. Front brings it to the top, Back pushes it behind others.

⧉
Copying Items

Select an item and click the ⧉ copy button in the control bar to place a duplicate of it on the canvas.

🟢
Grass & Hardscape Areas — Adding & Editing Dots

Grass and hardscape items (gravel, mulch, etc.) use a polygon shape to define their coverage area. Here's how to edit it:

  • Click the item to select it — colored dots appear at its corners.
  • Click on the canvas (anywhere not on an existing dot) to add a new dot and reshape the area.
  • Drag any dot to move it and adjust the shape.
  • Right-click a dot to delete it.
  • The ↻ button in the control bar resets the shape back to a rectangle.

The area covered and the cost update automatically as you reshape.

📏
Calibrating Scale

To get accurate area and material calculations, click "📏 Calibrate Scale from Known Distance" in the Materials panel:

  • Step 1: Click the start of something with a known length in your photo (a fence panel, car, door, etc.).
  • Step 2: Click the end of that same object.
  • Step 3: Enter or select the real-world length in feet, then click Apply.

Once calibrated, all grass, gravel, and path calculations use your actual yard dimensions.

📐
Total Area Input

If you know your yard's total area, type it (in square feet) into the "📐 Total area" field at the top of the Materials panel. This improves the accuracy of material quantity calculations even without scale calibration.

🗑️
Deleting Items

Select an item and click the 🗑️ trash button in the control bar, or press the Delete key on your keyboard.

💾
Saving Your Project

Click Save in the header to save your design to the cloud. The first time you'll be asked to name your project. After that, every save updates the same project automatically. Changes are also auto-saved every 5 seconds.

Click 📂 Projects to see all your saved projects, open a previous one, or start a new blank canvas.
